# Newsletter

Figma "Newsletter": the sign-up band above the footer on every page.

In Drupal it is the `newsletter` Webform, placed in the Newsletter region and themed by `templates/form/webform--newsletter.html.twig`. The heading is the webform title and the intro is its description, so both are editable under Structure » Webforms.

The form is not connected to an email platform yet (TAD §11, open question 1), and it stores no submissions.
