# Cards

Figma "Section · This week" (Home) and "Section · People" (About): a section heading (eyebrow, title, optional "All news" link and intro), then two or three story cards. Three cards share the 1280px column; two cards split it in half. On mobile the cards become a horizontal swipe track.

The "Cards" paragraph (`content_grid`) feeds it either hand-picked items or a listing (view).
