# Page title

Figma "Page title": the linen band at the top of listing and landing pages (Brands, News, Visit, Contact, policies, articles).

- `title`: Display XL, centred.
- `intro`: Body L, up to 620px wide.
- `breadcrumb` slot: desktop only. In Drupal pass `drupal_block('system_breadcrumb_block')`.
- `logo` slot: the press article variant shows the publication logo above the title.
